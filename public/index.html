NavalGame

2 players · 10 × 10 grid · 5 ships

Sink the enemy fleet

Create a room, send the code to a friend, hide your ships and take turns firing. First fleet to go under loses.

How to play
  1. Each player hides five ships on a 10 × 10 grid: carrier (5), battleship (4), cruiser (3), submarine (3) and destroyer (2).
  2. Ships go horizontally or vertically and cannot overlap.
  3. Players take turns firing one shot at a cell of the enemy grid. You learn whether it was a miss, a hit, or sank a ship.
  4. The first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins. If a player leaves or stays disconnected for two minutes, the other wins.
