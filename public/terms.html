NavalGame is a free, two-player naval battle played in the browser at naval.cloudils.com. By using it you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

1. The service

NavalGame lets two people play a naval battle in a private room identified by a six-character code. There are no accounts, no payments and no advertising. The game is provided free of charge for personal, non-commercial entertainment.

2. Acceptable use

When you use NavalGame you agree not to:

  • disrupt the service, for example by flooding it with requests, automating room creation or trying to exhaust its resources;
  • try to access rooms, data or systems you are not meant to reach, or to bypass its security or rate limits;
  • use the game for any unlawful purpose.

If you find a security problem, please report it privately to info@cloudils.com instead of exploiting it.

3. Data and privacy

NavalGame is designed to collect as little as possible:

  • Nickname. Shown to your opponent and kept with the room while it exists. Rooms are deleted automatically after 60 minutes without activity.
  • Seat token. A random code that lets you rejoin your seat after a reload. Your browser keeps it in session storage for that tab; the server keeps only a one-way hash of it, deleted with the room.
  • Remembered nickname. Your browser's local storage keeps your last nickname so you do not have to type it again. You can clear it from your browser at any time.
  • IP address. Used only to enforce limits on how many rooms and connections can be opened per minute. It is stored as a one-way hash for at most one minute.
  • Infrastructure. The game runs on Cloudflare, which processes network requests to deliver and protect the site, keeps short-term operational logs, and provides aggregate, cookie-free visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies.

The game itself sets no cookies, does not sell or share your data, and does not build profiles. For questions about your data, write to info@cloudils.com.
